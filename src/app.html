    AI settings

    Choose the AI that suggests edits and reviews photos.
    What the AI sees: a 1024 px preview and the photo's measurements, sent only to the provider you pick. Local models keep everything on your PC.
    What it changes: only slider values. Your photo is still processed by the app's own standard adjustments; no pixels are generated. Exported files contain no metadata at all: no AI or C2PA tags, no GPS, no camera data.

    Working

    Install on iPhone

    Adds Lumen Layer to your home screen.
    1. Tap the Share button in Safari: the square with an arrow pointing up.
    2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
    3. Tap Add. Open Lumen Layer from your home screen: it runs full screen and works offline.
